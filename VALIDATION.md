Validated on 4 November 2026 using automated JavaScript checks and a headless Chromium browser at a 390 × 844 phone viewport.

Passed checks:

- Strict 35-point selection, including fewer than four qualifiers and tied highest scorers below 35.
- Correct ticket lengths, distinct horses and KingMick positional restrictions.
- Combination counts: Exacta KingMick 4; Trifecta KingMick Top 4 = 10; Top 5 = 19; First Four KingMick = 57; boxed Top 5 Trifecta = 60; boxed Top 6 First Four = 360.
- All 12 Win/Place multis: hits, misses, partial results, unknown place terms and void races.
- Straight/Boxed/KingMick navigation, variant selection, expandable tickets and performance filters.
- Sequential result validation, duplicate finishers, edited results and persistence after reload.
- Full two-tipster entry, save/review and V3 snapshot generation.
- Comparison with snapshots generated by the original uploaded V2 code: historical snapshots remain unchanged and original selections remain visible.
- Missing historical snapshots are labelled reconstructed; invalid legacy boxed tickets are excluded from performance rates.
- Updated offline cache, cached assets and offline page reload.
- No page JavaScript errors during the browser checks and no horizontal overflow in the tested performance view.

These checks used synthetic fixtures, not real race outcomes. Real bookmaker settlement exceptions are outside automated assessment. The user's GitHub deployment and existing on-device data were not accessed or changed.
